The Problem

Care providers shouldn't operate like this.

Fragmented systems, manual compliance, disconnected workforce data, and spreadsheet-driven reporting have become accepted as normal. Every disconnected workflow creates more administration, more risk, and less time for delivering care.

The Solution

Sognos unifies demand, workforce and outcomes on a single operational platform.

Built on Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Copilot, Sognos connects intake, care planning, workforce coordination, compliance and reporting into one continuous workflow.

  1. 1

    Unified Care Records

    Referrals, assessments, care plans, incidents, service agreements and progress notes in one system.

  2. 2

    Built-In Compliance

    Audit trails, funding controls and evidence captured automatically as work happens.

  3. 3

    Connected Workforce

    SognosCare and SognosRoster share the same data layer across visits, plans and participant history.

  4. 4

    Real-Time Reporting

    Operational, workforce, compliance and funding reports generated continuously, not manually assembled.
