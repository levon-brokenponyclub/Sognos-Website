# Sognos — Brand Intelligence 
---

### What is Sognos?

Sognos is a **Microsoft-native software product and implementation company** based in Australia (Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland, Ahmedabad). They are a certified Microsoft Solutions Partner with a Business Applications designation.

They sit at the intersection of two things:
1. **Products** — Pre-built, purpose-built software platforms for specific service industries (SognosCare, SognosRoster, SognosGenogram)
2. **Solutions** — Implementation and configuration services across the broader Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Platform ecosystem

Their own positioning (from the Flourish case study back cover, which is the clearest single statement):
> "Healthcare first. Field services always. AI at the centre."

---

### What do they do?

They help organisations that deliver **complex, people-centred services** replace their broken, legacy systems with a unified Microsoft platform — so frontline workers spend less time on admin and more time on the actual work.

**Three products:**

| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **SognosCare** | End-to-end care management: scheduling, case management, compliance, billing, reporting. 4 editions: Disability & Mental Health, Allied Health, Support at Home, Residential Aged Care |
| **SognosRoster** | Workforce scheduling and optimisation for complex field environments |
| **SognosGenogram** | Visual relationship/family network mapping for child protection, foster care, mental health teams |

**Six solution areas:**
Field Service · CRM · Customer Experience · Customer Insights · Customer Service · Power Platform · QuickStart

---

### How do they do it?

1. **Build on Microsoft Dynamics 365 + Power Platform** — everything lives in the Microsoft cloud ecosystem, integrating with existing tools (HR, payroll, NDIS PRODA, finance)
2. **Configure, not code from scratch** — Sognos configures and customises Dynamics 365 modules to match each client's real operational workflows
3. **Phased delivery** — discovery → design → implementation → optimisation → ongoing evolution
4. **Mobile-first for frontline** — field workers and care staff can access, update and act on information from any device
5. **AI/Copilot embedded** — predictive scheduling, automated documentation, sentiment analysis, virtual agents
6. **Integration-first** — connects to NDIS PRODA, T1-HR, T1-Finance, Emplive, ArcGIS, EAM, and other operational systems

### How does it work (technically)?

1. Client engages Sognos for discovery and scoping
2. Sognos designs a Dynamics 365 configuration tailored to the client's operational model
3. Platform is built in phases — each phase delivers working functionality
4. Integrated with client's existing HR, finance, payroll, regulatory portals
5. Staff are trained; frontline workers get mobile app access
6. Managers get real-time dashboards and Power BI reporting
7. Ongoing: Sognos becomes strategic partner for continuous improvement
