# Flourish Australia — Healthcare Transformation

**Industry:** Mental Health / Community Services  
**Size:** 1,100+ staff  
**Scope:** National (Australia)

---

## The Challenge

- Legacy system reaching end of life  
- Heavy admin due to manual processes and duplicate data  
- Poor data quality impacting reporting and service delivery  
- Multiple disconnected systems across operations  

---

## The Solution

Sognos implemented a unified Dynamics 365 platform to manage:

- Enquiry → Intake → Support Delivery → Billing → Claiming  
- Integration with NDIS PRODA, HR, payroll, and finance systems  
- Real-time dashboards and reporting (Power BI)  
- Configurable workflows tailored to service delivery  

---

## The Impact

- 1,100+ users operating on a single system  
- Full operational visibility across services  
- Reduced manual admin and improved productivity  
- Scalable foundation for future growth
