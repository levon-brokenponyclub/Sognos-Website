## Core Capabilities

**Case Management**
Efficiently track and manage customer inquiries, complaints, and support requests with assignment, escalation, and resolution capabilities.

**Knowledge Resources**
Self-service materials including articles, FAQs, and troubleshooting guides reduce dependency on team intervention.

**Omnichannel Engagement**
Support across email, chat, social media, and phone ensures consistent, personalized experiences across communication channels.

**SLA Management**
Automated tracking of response and resolution times ensures compliance with service level agreements.

**Service Analytics**
Performance metrics including resolution times, satisfaction scores, productivity data, and issue trends guide operational improvements.

**Unified Service Desk**
Integrated interface connecting Dynamics 365 CRM, Outlook, and SharePoint for seamless customer information access.

**AI-Driven Insights**
Sentiment analysis, predictive analytics, and virtual agents automate routine tasks and anticipate customer needs.

**Customer Feedback Management**
Collection and analysis of surveys, ratings, and sentiment data inform satisfaction levels and improvement areas.

**Field Service Integration**
Seamless connection with Dynamics 365 Field Service extends capabilities to on-site operations.
