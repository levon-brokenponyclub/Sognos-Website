## What is SognosCare?

"SognosCare is an end-to-end specialised care management solution designed for healthcare and community service organisations."

Built on Microsoft Dynamics 365, the platform integrates scheduling, case management, compliance, billing, and reporting into one unified system. Using AI-driven insights and automation, it helps care teams prioritize patient care over administrative tasks.

---

## SognosCare Editions for Healthcare and Community Services

**Four Tailored Editions:**

### **Disability & Mental Health**
Purpose-built for NDIS and psychosocial support providers managing participant goals, funding plans, incident reporting, and NDIS Practice Standards compliance.

### **Allied HealthCare**
Designed for mobile therapy teams and community health providers. Simplifies scheduling, referral management, billing, and progress notes for physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and speech pathology services.

### **Support at Home**
Empowers home care providers with tools for scheduling, service delivery tracking, billing, and reporting to deliver personalized, compliant services.

### **Residential Aged Care**
Supports facility operations with resident care visibility, compliance tracking, staff rostering, and resource management in one system.
