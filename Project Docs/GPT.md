## 2. Problem Framing (Agitation)

**Section Title:**  
Your systems are slowing down the work that matters

**Body Copy:**  
Disconnected platforms, manual reporting, and outdated workflows are forcing your teams to spend more time managing systems than delivering care or service.

**Pain Points (Grid):**
- Duplicate data entry across multiple systems  
- No real-time visibility across operations  
- Compliance reporting takes days, not minutes  
- Scheduling managed through spreadsheets or calls  
- Frontline teams buried in admin  

**Transitional Line:**  
It’s not a people problem. It’s a systems problem.
