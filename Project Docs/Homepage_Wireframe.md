### Source: Brand Intelligence.md only

## PAGE NARRATIVE

The visitor is an Operations Director, IT Manager, or COO at a service organisation.
They have a broken or outdated system. They're under pressure from compliance, staff burnout, or growth.
They arrive on this page. The page must answer three things in order:

1. **Is this for me?** — Qualify fast
2. **What does it do?** — Show the platform
3. **Does it work?** — Prove it

Positioning line (Brand Intelligence): *"Healthcare first. Field services always. AI at the centre."*
